# Privacy

TSMux collects nothing about you. It has no analytics, no ads and no account
with us.

Each tailnet you add runs a Tailscale node on your device. That node talks to
the tailnet's coordination server (Tailscale's, or your own Headscale) and
sends it the same data the official Tailscale apps send, including diagnostic
logs: Tailscale's [privacy policy](https://tailscale.com/privacy-policy)
covers it. Sign-in happens on that server's web page, so TSMux never sees your
password.

Tailnet state, node keys and TSMux's own log stay on your device. Uninstalling
the app deletes them.
